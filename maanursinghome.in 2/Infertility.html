Infertility Among Couples

Infertility is defined by WHO as the inability to conceive after 12 months of regular, unprotected intercourse.

Infertility is rising globally among both men and women. At Maa Nursing Home and NetraJyoti Eyecare Centre, we offer a complete range of diagnostic and treatment options through our dedicated Infertility Clinic, led by Dr. Jyoti Shah — senior gynaecologist, obstetrician and infertility specialist.

Our highly trained IVF and fertility experts use advanced technology and strict safety protocols, ensuring some of the highest success rates in the country. Each couple receives a customized treatment plan tailored to their medical history, diagnosis and personal needs.

When Should You See a Doctor?

  • Unable to conceive after 1 year of regular unprotected intercourse
  • Issues with erection, ejaculation, pain or swelling in testicles
  • History of groin, testicle, penile or scrotal surgery
  • Female partner is over 35 years of age
  • Irregular menstrual cycles or severe period-related issues

Infertility must be evaluated in BOTH partners — the cause is not known until both are thoroughly examined.

Causes of Infertility

In Women

  • Ovulatory dysfunction
  • Endometriosis
  • Advanced maternal age
  • Luteal phase defects
  • Uterine or cervical abnormalities
  • Fibroids

In Men

  • Low sperm production
  • Poor sperm motility or abnormal morphology
  • Anti-sperm antibodies
  • Blockages preventing sperm release
  • Hormonal or chromosomal abnormalities
  • Chronic illnesses and infections
  • Lifestyle factors (alcohol, smoking, obesity, stress)
  • Low sperm count (<15 million per mL)

How Is Infertility Diagnosed?

Both partners undergo a comprehensive workup, including:

  • Medical history and physical examination
  • Assessment of menstrual cycle and ovulation
  • Semen analysis
  • Ultrasound (pelvic sonography)
  • Hormonal testing
  • Evaluation for erectile or ejaculation issues
  • Additional fertility imaging or laparoscopy/hysteroscopy if required

Infertility Treatments Offered at Maa Infertility Clinic

Depending on the underlying cause, we create a personalized treatment plan. Options include:

  • IUI (Intrauterine Insemination)
  • IVF & ICSI (Test-Tube Baby)
