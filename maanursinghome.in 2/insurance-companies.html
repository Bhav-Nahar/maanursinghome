Insurance & Cashless Facility

We are empanelled with all major health insurance companies and TPAs, including CGHS, to provide seamless and trusted cashless hospitalization services.

  1. Home

We are on the panel of all leading Health Insurance Companies and Third Party Administrators (TPAs). These organizations share a long-standing association with our hospital and trust us for delivering high-quality and ethical healthcare.

We are also empanelled with CGHS (Central Government Health Scheme), offering cashless facilities to employees of various public sector undertakings.

Our dedicated TPA Desk ensures a smooth and hassle-free process for Mediclaim and cashless hospitalization cases.
